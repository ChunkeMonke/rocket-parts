## Implications already visible (for Phase 1)
- A 29 mm motor in a 38 mm tube is **not** minimum-diameter. It needs a 29 mm motor mount
  (tube or printed into the fin can), centering, and motor retention. The 38 mm thrust ring
  in the library roadmap doesn't apply to this rocket.
- **Motor cost vs. case:** the H128W reload is listed at $34.99–$39.49, but a reload also
  needs an RMS-29/180 case, which the owner doesn't have. Phase 1 compares a single-use H
  (price not found yet) against borrowing or renting a case at the launch.
- Hazmat shipping on motors can be large next to a $100 budget. Phase 1 checks whether the
  club's launch-day vendor avoids it.
- The rest of the buy list (airframe, parachute, rail buttons, chute protection) gets
  priced in Phase 3/4. Kevlar is already owned.
